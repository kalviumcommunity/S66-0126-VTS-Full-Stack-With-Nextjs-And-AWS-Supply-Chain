# 🎨 Tribal Art Marketplace  
### Direct Marketplace Connecting Tribal Artists with Global Buyers

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Tribal and rural artists often depend on middlemen to sell their handmade products.

This leads to:

- ❌ Unfair pricing  
- ❌ Reduced income for artists  
- ❌ Lack of global exposure  
- ❌ Limited digital presence  
- ❌ Buyers not knowing the real source of products  

There is no centralized digital platform that directly connects tribal artists with global buyers while ensuring transparency and fair trade.

---

## 💡 Solution Overview

**Tribal Art Marketplace** is a web-based platform that connects tribal artists directly with buyers.

The platform enables:

- Artists to showcase their handmade products  
- Buyers to browse authentic tribal artworks  
- Category-based filtering of products  
- Transparent pricing  
- User authentication and dashboard access  

The goal is to eliminate middlemen and empower tribal communities through technology.

---

## 👥 Target Users

- Tribal and rural artists  
- Buyers interested in handmade crafts  
- Cultural art enthusiasts  
- Ethical product consumers

## 🚀 MVP (Minimum Viable Product)

The MVP will include:

- User Registration & Login
- Product Listing Page
- Product Cards (image, title, description)
- Category Filtering
- Dashboard Page
- Static About Page
- Contact Page
- Protected Routes

The MVP will be functional and demo-ready.

---

## ⚙️ Functional Requirements

- Users can register and log in.
- Artists can add products.
- Users can view product listings.
- Users can filter products by category.
- Dashboard displays user information.
- Only authenticated users can access dashboard.
- Products display title, description, and image.

---

## 🔒 Non-Functional Requirements

- API response time < 500ms
- Secure password hashing
- JWT-based authentication
- Responsive design
- Clean and consistent UI
- Scalable backend structure
- Data consistency with no duplication
